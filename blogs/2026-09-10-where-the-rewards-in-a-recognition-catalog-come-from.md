Cloud APIs for mobile top ups A determined platform publishes 200 million reward options across 100 countries and 130 currencies. It does not own them. Employee recognition platforms source almost none of their catalog directly: they buy access from reward infrastructure providers, then curate, localize and route what those suppliers already carry.
